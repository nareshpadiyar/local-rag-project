ACMECloud Private Cloud - Platform Overview
Environment: Production
Primary regions: us-east-1, eu-west-1, ap-south-1
Platform services: Compute, Storage, Network, IAM, Monitoring.
Compute provisioning target: 15 minutes for standard VM requests.
Critical incident response target: acknowledge within 15 minutes and engage the on-call engineer.
Backup retention: 35 days for production databases.
Maintenance window: Sunday 01:00-04:00 UTC.
Escalation: Service Desk -> Platform SRE -> Service Owner -> Incident Commander.

Known issue KC-104: VM provisioning can remain in QUEUED when the image-store worker has stale queue state.
Workaround: verify image-store worker health, inspect active transfer jobs, and restart the worker only after confirming no active transfer is progressing.
